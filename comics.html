Fate Doesn't Listen

Chosen by an ancient force of light, Nathaniel Brooks, a reluctant teenage boy, must set out to a hidden magical world to face the light's corrupted twin before it regains its physical form.

Fate Doesn't Listen is a comic that, after two years as an idea, has taken shape as a webcomic series. I started drawing it in September 2025. The first episode went online on February 6th 2026, and new episodes are posted regularly every Friday at 5.00 pm.

Read it here!

Sky & Sea

Sky & Sea is a story where an explorer and a magical sea creature come across a lonely sailor and must work together to bring the sea creature safely home before the malicious state of Eruspo can get hold of her.

This series is currently in development.
